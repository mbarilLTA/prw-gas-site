The route

Eight stops, one city's story

Victorian grandeur, tobacco-lord mansions and the medieval heart of Glasgow, in the order you'll walk them.

  1. Royal Exchange Square

    Meet beside the Duke of Wellington and his famous traffic cone, outside the Gallery of Modern Art, once a tobacco lord's mansion.

  2. Buchanan Street

    Glasgow's grand shopping street and a crash course in reading Victorian façades.

  3. George Square & the City Chambers

    The civic heart of the city, and the marble-clad chambers Queen Victoria opened in 1888.

  4. Trades Hall

    Robert Adam's only surviving building in Glasgow, home of the city's craft guilds since 1794.

  5. The Merchant City

    The streets the tobacco lords built, and the fortunes and darker history behind them.

  6. Glasgow Cross

    The old centre of town, watched over by the 17th-century Tolbooth Steeple.

  7. High Street

    Glasgow's oldest street and the original home of its university. This is the uphill half-mile.

  8. Glasgow Cathedral

    The medieval cathedral that came through the Reformation intact, built over the tomb of St Mungo.

Wheelchair accessible; the last half-mile from Glasgow Cross is uphill. No planned toilet break, though toilets are on the route.

Do I need to book?

Yes. Book online by card or Apple Pay, right up to the start time. Cash can't be taken on the street.

What if it rains?

The tour runs rain or shine, with sheltered stops on wet days. Check the forecast and wrap up. Tours are cancelled only in truly horrendous weather.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, with 24 hours' notice (48 hours for groups of 6 or more), as a refund or a move to another date.

Is it suitable for children?

Everyone is welcome and children 12 and under go free. The content suits ages 10 and up best. Bookings must be made by an adult.

Is it accessible?

Generally, yes: it's wheelchair accessible, with an uphill last half-mile. Get in touch beforehand and Johnny will check it suits you.

What if I'm running late?

Call or WhatsApp 07516 554828 and Johnny will do his best to help you catch up with the group.

What if I can't afford it?

Get in touch and we'll work something out.
